**Octopus pattern (experimental, established 2026-11-21, piloted successfully):** generalizes the validated big-brain pattern. Phase 1: middle-manager makes a coarse pass (reads the plan, frames constraints) and dispatches workers, specialists, AND the director in parallel — the director produces its own independent findings during phase 1. Phase 2: on first-pass completion, the director reviews all worker reports in light of its own findings and the original request, then issues rulings plus revision/next-step briefs. Phase 3: revisions go back to the ORIGINAL worker sessions (sessionID reuse preserves context); the director may rule phases unnecessary. Phase 4: middle-manager runs an intensive final compile. Pilot lesson: the director's falsifiable-claims audit caught its own wrong predictions — always require explicit falsifiable predictions in phase 1.

**Why:** delegation learnings were scattered across four memories with an internal contradiction and an unmoderated "never" billing claim; the 2026-11-21 study added empirical per-model scores, Jesse's DeepSeek-over-GPT preference, and the named-agent fleet's actual configurations.
**How to apply:** consult this matrix for any unspecified-model subagent request; use the octopus pattern for multi-angle research/design tasks; keep worker briefs tight (3–6 deliverables, explicit do-NOT-redo list, one open question max). When dispatching the named specialists, remember their models are pre-configured — pass question + IDs only, no model parameter.
